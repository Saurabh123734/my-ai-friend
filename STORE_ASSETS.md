GoKart Dodge Pro — Store assets checklist and templates

This file describes the assets and metadata needed to publish to app stores (Google Play, Apple App Store, Microsoft Store, Steam, itch.io).

1) App title and descriptions
- Title: GoKart Dodge Pro
- Short description (80 chars max for Google Play): "Family-friendly gokart dodge game — career, challenges, and pro levels."
- Full description (up to 4000 chars): Write a friendly overview highlighting career, drivers, gokarts, dodge mechanics, daily challenges, achievements, and PWA/native installability.

2) Visual assets (prepare at minimum PNG, high quality):
- App icon (adaptive / square) at multiple sizes (recommended):
  - 512×512 (Google Play main icon)
  - 1024×1024 (App Store)
  - 192×192, 144×144, 96×96, 48×48 for web/PWA
- Feature/badge images: 1024×500 (Google Play feature graphic)
- Promotional video (optional): 30–60s MP4 (720p or 1080p)
- Screenshots (phone/tablet/desktop):
  - Mobile: 1080×1920 (portrait) or 1920×1080 (landscape). Provide 4–8 screenshots showing gameplay, driver/gokart selection, career screen, abilities, daily challenges.
  - Tablet: 1200×1920
  - Desktop (for Steam/store pages): 1280×720 or 1920×1080

3) Store-specific assets and notes
- Google Play: 512×512 icon, 1024×500 feature graphic, screenshots, privacy policy URL, contact email
- Apple App Store: 1024×1024 icon, up to 10 screenshots per device type, privacy policy URL, support URL, age rating information
- Microsoft Store: 448×448 tile icon + screenshots, promotional images, privacy policy
- Steam: header image (460×215), capsule images (616×353, 231×87), screenshots, trailer video
- itch.io: ZIP of web build or desktop builds; cover image 640×360 recommended

4) Metadata & policy
- Privacy policy (hosted URL). Include data collection, ads, analytics, and child data policy if kids are targeted.
- Support/contact email and a support URL
- Age rating/target audience (indicate family-friendly, suitable for ages up to 100 years obviously)
- Any third-party libraries or assets licensing information

5) Suggested filenames & folder
- /store-assets/
  - icon-1024.png
  - icon-512.png
  - feature-1024x500.png
  - screenshot-mobile-1.png ... screenshot-mobile-6.png
  - steam-header.png
  - promo-video.mp4
  - privacy-policy.txt (source)
  - store-description.txt

6) Quick tips
- Use transparent or rounded corners as required by stores when exporting icons.
- Keep text in screenshots minimal and localized copies for major languages if possible.
- Test the app bundle on a real device before publishing.
